# Roadmap and Contribution Paths

The project advances through evidence gates, not feature volume.

## P0 — deterministic contract and falsification

- close state-transition and validation contracts;
- separate and freeze the minimum EmptyBench corpus and oracle;
- complete the supported-runtime and installed-package matrix;
- retain exact vectors, digests, contract identifiers, and limitations; and
- run an independent adversarial review against one stable revision.

## P1 — governed adapter evidence

- implement one read-only adapter against replayable, non-sensitive fixtures;
- empirically test its population, access, pagination, error, freshness, and
  finality profile under explicit authority; and
- run approved discovery and shadow evaluation without granting write access.

## Research gate

Run a preregistered comparative campaign. Continue only if the frozen results
meet the declared unsupported-negative reduction and valid-negative retention
thresholds without reinterpretation.

## Later contracts

Authenticated issuers/adapters, trusted time, monotonic registry heads, replay
controls, signing, revocation distribution, and multi-source composition are
separate versioned decisions—not implied by P0.

## Ways to contribute

- file a **research gap** with competing prior art and a falsifiable question;
- submit a **matched benchmark case** with a visible-control/fault pair;
- propose a **contract change** with compatibility and threat analysis;
- reproduce a named revision and publish exact environment and output evidence;
- challenge a claim or trust boundary; or
- improve documentation without strengthening unsupported claims.

Use the repository’s structured
[issue forms](https://github.com/redxking/evidence-state-io/issues/new/choose)
and track accepted work in
[GitHub Projects](https://github.com/redxking/evidence-state-io/projects).
