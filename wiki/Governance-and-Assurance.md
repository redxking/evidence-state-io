# Governance and assurance

Architecture changes use versioned ADRs. Contract changes require explicit
version boundaries, negative tests, compatibility analysis, and updated
traceability. Claims must distinguish proposed, implemented, locally tested,
CI verified, externally reproduced, and production-authorized states.

Apache-2.0 governs source use; DCO sign-off records contribution provenance.
Maintainers review security-sensitive and contract changes. Synthetic local
evidence is never relabeled as independent validation. See
[GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/redxking/evidence-state-io/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md)
and [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/redxking/evidence-state-io/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
