# Roadmap

P0 closes the deterministic single-source contract, local acceptance, public
documentation, browser parity, and release custody. The next research gate is
to freeze and preregister an implementation-owned campaign with separately
controlled oracle custody, then seek external reproduction.

Authenticated registry and adapter evidence follow if benchmark and discovery
gates justify continued investment. Multi-source composition remains rejected
until its coverage and trust semantics are specified and approved. A read-only
adapter vertical proceeds only after the benchmark gate—not as a substitute for
it. See [Roadmap and Contribution Paths](Roadmap-and-Contribution-Paths).
