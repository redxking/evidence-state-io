# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a rigorous, respectful, and inclusive research and engineering
community. Participation is welcome across experience levels and disciplines when it
advances the work without compromising another person's safety, dignity, privacy, or
ability to contribute.

## Expected behavior

- Engage the argument and evidence, not the person.
- Distinguish verified results, assumptions, judgments, proposals, and uncertainty.
- Give credit, preserve provenance, and correct the record when evidence changes.
- Invite technically grounded disagreement and make review criteria explicit.
- Respect privacy, disclosure boundaries, and the authority of system and data owners.
- Accept maintainer decisions on scope while remaining free to document a reasoned dissent.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, discrimination, or sexualized conduct.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, trolling, or deliberately misleading claims.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing credentials, exploit details, sensitive fixtures, or private-system information
  through public project channels.
- Using project access or research artifacts to exceed authorization, target third parties,
  or imply operational approval that has not been granted.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project boards, wiki
pages, community events, and other spaces where a person represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report ordinary conduct concerns to the maintainer through a private coordination channel.
If the report includes a vulnerability, sensitive data, or exploit detail, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not ask the subject of a report to investigate it.

The maintainer will review reports as privately and impartially as practical, may request
additional evidence, and may remove content, limit participation, reject contributions, or
ban an account when necessary. Retaliation against a reporter or reviewer is prohibited.
There is no guaranteed response time, but immediate safety and disclosure risks take
priority.
